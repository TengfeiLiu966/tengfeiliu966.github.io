I am currently a Lecturer at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing University of Technology (BJUT). I received my Ph.D. degree from BJUT in June 2025 under the supervision of Prof. [Yongli Hu](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/author/37420507000), and conducted my doctoral research in the group led by Prof. [Baocai Yin](https://www.bjut.edu.cn/info/1059/1568.htm). From February 2024 to February 2025, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the ReLER Lab, University of Technology Sydney (UTS), under the supervision of Prof. [Xiaojun Chang](https://www.xiaojun.ai/) and Prof. [Hanyu Gu](https://profiles.uts.edu.au/Hanyu.Gu). My research interests include multimodal learning, medical image analysis, trustworthy large language models, and multi-agent systems. I have published more than 20 peer-reviewed papers, including 14 papers as first or corresponding author, in journals and conferences such as IEEE TNNLS/TMM/TCSVT/TASLP/TKDD, Neural Networks, CVPR, and AAAI. I am the principal investigator of the National Postdoctoral Researcher Program (Category C), the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation General Program, and the Beijing Natural Science Foundation Youth Program, and have participated in several national-level research projects. I have received honors including the National Scholarship for Doctoral Students, CSC Scholarship, Outstanding Graduate Award, and Top 100 Graduates Award.
